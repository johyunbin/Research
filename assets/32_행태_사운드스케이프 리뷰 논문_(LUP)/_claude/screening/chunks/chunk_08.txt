Birds perform behavioral adjustments when confronted with atypical environmental situations (e.g. urban noises, artificial light, human voice or vegetation loss). The aim of this study was to investigate vocal adjustments of Masked Gnatcatcher (Polioptila dumicola) males in environments located near a route with varying level of traffic noise (measured in decibels) and differing percentage of cover vegetation.
