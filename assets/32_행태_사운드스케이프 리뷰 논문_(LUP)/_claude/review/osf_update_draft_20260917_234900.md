## 0. Justification For Update

This update aligns the registration with how the review was actually conducted, as reported in the manuscript prepared for submission. It records the completed review stage, the software used, how citation tracking and the OpenAlex supplementary search were run, screening and extraction procedures as carried out, changes to quality appraisal, the four meta-analysed clusters and the analysis rules applied, the sensitivity analyses performed, registered procedures that were not carried out, and the one analysis component added after registration (planning evidence strength). The original registration remains available unchanged, and the same deviations are reported in the manuscript.

## 1. Review Methods › Type of review

Update (September 2026): Four clusters met the rule of at least three comparable effect sizes and were meta-analysed: walking speed, staying (dwell time), social interaction, and sound–behaviour correlations (16 contributing studies). Space use/visitation could not be pooled because the studies did not report variance information.

## 2. Review Methods › Current review stage

Update (September 2026): Searches, screening, full-text assessment, citation tracking, screening of the OpenAlex supplementary set, data extraction, quality appraisal, synthesis, and meta-analyses have been completed, and the manuscript has been prepared for submission.

## 3. Review Methods › Software

Update (September 2026): Rayyan and ASReview were not used; records, screening decisions, and extracted data were managed with Python scripts and CSV files. The meta-analyses were run in Python 3.13 (NumPy 2.2, SciPy 1.17) with a purpose-written implementation of REML estimation and the Hartung–Knapp adjustment; R (metafor) was not used.

## 4. Review Methods › Overlapping authorships

Update (September 2026): The review has a single author. One study by the author (Jo & Jeon, 2020) met the inclusion criteria and was included in the narrative synthesis; it did not contribute to any meta-analysis. Because there was no second reviewer, its screening and extraction decisions were not cross-checked by another person.

## 5. Search Strategy › Other search strategies

Update (September 2026): Citation tracking was run once through OpenAlex, using as seeds the 84 studies that the database searches yielded as included or sensitivity-only; studies added through citation tracking or the OpenAlex search were not themselves tracked. It returned 2,073 records not already in the pool (413 backward, 1,660 forward). Because most were off-topic, a title-level automated prefilter that was not specified at registration was applied: a record was kept for manual screening when its title contained terms from at least two of the three registered concept blocks and it was not an animal or bioacoustic study (428 records), and the other 1,645 records were excluded at title level. This prefilter can miss eligible studies whose titles do not mention behaviour. The OpenAlex supplementary set was screened on 6 August 2026, after an internal audit found that it had not yet been screened: after duplicates were removed, 329 records were screened, 15 were sought for full text, 5 were assessed, and 2 were included. The reference lists of adjacent reviews were not screened.

## 6. Search Strategy › Results of contacting authors

Update (September 2026): No authors were contacted. Studies whose effect-size ingredients could not be recovered from the published report were kept in the narrative synthesis and excluded from the meta-analyses.

## 7. Screening › Screening stages

Update (September 2026): Full texts could not be obtained for 89 of the 189 records sought from the database searches (84 of them rated uncertain at title/abstract screening); these records were not assessed. Full-text assessment followed the same AI-assisted procedure as title/abstract screening, with every assessment read by the author.

## 8. Screening › Screener instructions

Update (September 2026): Recurring boundary cases at full-text assessment were resolved with rules applied to all such records. Studies relating residential noise exposure to physical activity not tied to a specific public space were excluded under the setting criterion. Studies reporting only behavioural intentions were classified as sensitivity-only, as registered. Reverse-direction studies whose outcomes were the perception or assessment of the soundscape were included, consistent with the registered reverse-direction outcomes. Studies in which the acoustic environment entered only as a control variable were excluded. In the citation-tracking branch, laboratory or VR reproductions of outdoor scenes were also classified as sensitivity-only, whereas in the database branch such studies stayed in the main synthesis; none of them contributed to any meta-analysis. Language eligibility was confirmed from the full text rather than from index metadata, because some records indexed as English were published in Chinese, Korean, or Japanese.

## 9. Screening › Screening reliability

Update (September 2026): The author read every AI pre-classification (1,316 title/abstract records and all full-text assessments); no classification was changed. The planned re-screening of a random 10% subsample after an interval was not carried out, so intra-rater consistency was not quantified.

## 10. Extraction › Extraction stages

Update (September 2026): No separate pilot on 10 studies was run. Effect statistics were extracted verbatim together with their location in the source text, and conversion to effect sizes was done only at the synthesis stage.

## 11. Extraction › Extraction reliability

Update (September 2026): The AI-assisted extraction was read and accepted by the author. The planned value-by-value check against the source PDFs and the 10% re-extraction were not carried out. Errors in effect-size inputs found later during the review of the analysis code were corrected against the source texts (see Synthesis reliability).

## 12. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Planned data transformations

Update (September 2026): Detailed effect-size rules were fixed in a separate analysis-rules document before effect sizes were computed. When a study provided several effects for the same analysis, one was selected by priority (objective measure, primary analysis, full sample), and effects of equal priority were combined within the study assuming a correlation of 0.5. Only one study per shared sample was used. For repeated observations, the number of participants rather than observations was used as the sample size, except in two social-interaction studies that reported only video-excerpt units, where those units were used. Odds ratios were converted to d with Chinn's method. Missing group sizes were assumed equal, missing standard deviations were recovered from test statistics or standard errors, and variances were derived from reported confidence intervals where sample sizes were not reported; a sensitivity analysis excluded the inputs that relied on assumed group sizes or recovered test statistics.

## 13. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Missing data

Update (September 2026): Authors were not contacted (see Results of contacting authors).

## 14. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Quality assessment

Update (September 2026): RoB 2 was not applied. Full-text reading identified only two randomised experiments, too few for a separate risk-of-bias subset, and their weaknesses are reported through the MMAT criteria for randomised controlled trials. MMAT categories were assigned from the full text rather than from design labels in the extraction sheet. Studies were rated high (4–5 criteria met), moderate (3), or low (0–2). Thirteen studies from citation tracking whose text had been truncated during automated text extraction were re-appraised from the complete full text; five changed rating (four upward, one downward), and the re-appraised ratings are used.

## 15. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Synthesis plan

Update (September 2026): Random-effects models estimated tau-squared by REML and applied the Hartung–Knapp adjustment to the confidence intervals; 95% prediction intervals are also reported. The narrative synthesis is reported as study counts by behavioural domain and sound-source category (evidence map), by direction of the studied relationship, and by behavioural measurement method; a harvest plot and vote counting of effect directions for studies outside the meta-analyses were not produced. The planned moderator and subgroup analyses (setting type, study design, behavioural measurement method, and direction) were not performed because no cluster had more than six effect sizes. An analysis component not in the registration was added: exposures manipulated or measured in the included studies were grouped into planning levers, each given a planning evidence strength (high, moderate, low, very low) based on the number of contributing studies, their MMAT ratings, whether pooled confidence intervals excluded zero, the diversity of settings, and robustness in sensitivity analyses. This is a pragmatic judgement by the single author, not a GRADE certainty-of-evidence assessment.

## 16. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Criteria for conclusions / inference criteria

Update (September 2026): Results are reported with effect sizes, 95% confidence and prediction intervals, and two-sided p values, and the manuscript describes pooled estimates whose confidence intervals exclude zero (p < 0.05) as statistically significant.

## 17. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Synthesis reliability

Update (September 2026): There were no co-authors, so no co-author review of the analysis outputs took place. After the analyses were completed, the analysis code was reviewed for errors with an AI-based code review. The review found four errors: tau-squared estimated by maximum likelihood instead of REML, two effects from one study entered separately in the walking-speed cluster despite the one-effect-per-study rule, two input values in the correlation cluster that did not match the source studies, and a rank-correlation sensitivity analysis that excluded nothing. All were corrected and all meta-analyses were recomputed; the corrections did not change the conclusions.

## 18. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Publication bias analyses

Update (September 2026): No meta-analysed cluster reached 10 effect sizes (the largest had six), so funnel plots, Egger's test, and trim-and-fill were not performed, and publication bias was not assessed further.

## 19. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Sensitivity analyses / robustness checks

Update (September 2026): Performed: (1) excluding MMAT low-quality studies (not possible for walking speed, where all contributing studies were rated low); (2) excluding laboratory or VR reproductions of outdoor scenes (identical to the main analyses, because none contributed); (4) leave-one-out for each cluster. Added: recomputing sample sizes by observation units, excluding rank correlations, excluding reconstructed inputs, an alternative walking-speed definition that adds a plaza background-music study, and excluding studies found by citation tracking. Added after the main analyses: adding a study that reported visit frequency instead of dwell time (staying) and adding a study whose observation records were not independent (social interaction). Not performed: (3) including behavioural-intention-only studies, and (5) the fixed-effect versus random-effects comparison.
