## 0. Justification For Update (등록본 맨 위에 표시됨)

This update records implementation details and deviations from the registered protocol that are already final: the current review stage, the software actually used, how citation tracking and the OpenAlex supplementary search were carried out, the boundary rules and language checks applied at full-text screening, changes to quality appraisal, the meta-analytic estimator, and planned analyses that could not be performed because there were too few effect sizes. The original registration remains available unchanged. Deviations that depend on work still in progress (the narrative synthesis, the composition of the meta-analytic clusters, the remaining sensitivity analyses, and the screening and extraction reliability checks) will be recorded in a further update before manuscript submission. All deviations are also reported in the manuscript.

## 1. Review Methods › Current review stage (덧붙임)

Update (17 September 2026): Title/abstract and full-text screening, citation tracking, screening of the OpenAlex supplementary set, data extraction, quality appraisal, and the meta-analyses have been completed. The remaining synthesis and the manuscript are in preparation.

## 2. Review Methods › Software (덧붙임)

Update (17 September 2026): Rayyan and ASReview were not used; records, screening decisions, and extracted data were managed with Python scripts and CSV files. The meta-analyses were run in Python 3.13 (NumPy 2.2, SciPy 1.17) with a purpose-written implementation of REML estimation and the Hartung–Knapp adjustment; R (metafor) was not used.

## 3. Search Strategy › Other search strategies (덧붙임)

Update (17 September 2026): Citation tracking was run once through OpenAlex, using as seeds the 84 studies that the database searches yielded as included or sensitivity-only; studies added through citation tracking or the OpenAlex search were not themselves tracked. It returned 2,073 records not already in the pool (413 backward, 1,660 forward). Because most of them were off-topic, a title-level automated prefilter that was not specified at registration was applied: a record was kept for manual screening when its title contained terms from at least two of the three registered concept blocks and it was not an animal or bioacoustic study (428 records), and the other 1,645 records were excluded at title level. This prefilter can miss eligible studies whose titles do not mention behaviour. The OpenAlex supplementary set was screened on 6 August 2026, after an internal audit found that it had not yet been screened: after duplicates were removed, 329 records were screened, 15 were sought for full text, 5 were assessed, and 2 were included.

## 4. Screening › Screener instructions (덧붙임)

Update (17 September 2026): Recurring boundary cases at full-text assessment were resolved with rules applied to all such records. Studies relating residential noise exposure to physical activity not tied to a specific public space were excluded under the setting criterion. Studies reporting only behavioural intentions were classified as sensitivity-only, as registered. Reverse-direction studies whose outcomes were the perception or assessment of the soundscape were included, consistent with the registered reverse-direction outcomes. Studies in which the acoustic environment entered only as a control variable were excluded. In the citation-tracking branch, laboratory or VR reproductions of outdoor scenes were also classified as sensitivity-only, whereas in the database branch such studies stayed in the main synthesis; none of them contributed to any meta-analysis. Language eligibility was confirmed from the full text rather than from index metadata, because some records indexed as English were published in Chinese, Korean, or Japanese.

## 5. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Quality assessment (덧붙임)

Update (17 September 2026): RoB 2 was not applied. Full-text reading identified only two randomised experiments, too few for a separate risk-of-bias subset, and their weaknesses are reported through the MMAT criteria for randomised controlled trials. MMAT categories were assigned from the full text rather than from design labels in the extraction sheet. Studies were rated high (4–5 criteria met), moderate (3), or low (0–2). Thirteen studies from citation tracking whose text had been truncated during automated text extraction were re-appraised from the complete full text; five changed rating (four upward, one downward), and the re-appraised ratings are used.

## 6. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Synthesis plan (덧붙임)

Update (17 September 2026): Random-effects models estimate tau-squared by REML and apply the Hartung–Knapp adjustment to the confidence intervals; 95% prediction intervals are also reported. The planned moderator and subgroup analyses (setting type, study design, behavioural measurement method, and direction) were not performed because no cluster had more than six effect sizes.

## 7. Synthesis And Quality Assessment › Publication bias analyses (덧붙임)

Update (17 September 2026): No meta-analysed cluster reached 10 effect sizes, so funnel plots, Egger's test, and trim-and-fill were not performed.
